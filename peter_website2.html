I study how psychedelics and meditation change the brain, and what these altered states might offer for treating disease and enhancing well-being.

I completed an MSc in Neuroscience at the University of Copenhagen, using fMRI to map how LSD and music shape brain activity. I have since completed a second master’s at the Meditation Research Program at Harvard Medical School and Massachusetts General Hospital, investigating the neural correlates of advanced meditation with EEG, and lived in Boston from April 2025 to May 2026 for that work. I also hold a BSc in Pharmacy and have conducted behavioural neuropharmacology research on MDMA.

Alongside this, I develop and write psychedelic-informed guides for cluster headache prevention with ClusterFree.
